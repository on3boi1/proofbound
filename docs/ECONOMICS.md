# Economics

Balances use integer yoctoNEAR. The default 0.16 NEAR mint deposit divides into 0.05 storage, 0.10 prepaid compute and 0.01 platform fee. One Fuel unit is funded by 10¹⁸ yoctoNEAR. Each job includes 0.02 NEAR for retained records. The default service fee is 0.01 NEAR.

Private jobs reserve existing Fuel. Job completion releases unused units. Marketplace jobs escrow the service fee and maximum compute allowance. A successful settlement splits service 90/10 and credits unused compute to the customer. Failed paid work refunds all service and compute. The operator covers failed inference separately.

Earn-to-Refill converts the accepted owner percentage to funded whole Fuel units. Remainder stays claimable. The buyer receives transferable Fuel and history. Prior earnings stay claimable by the former owner. Provider charges draw from external NEAR AI credits. Demo balances are simulated or testnet only.
